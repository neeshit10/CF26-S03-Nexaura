## Universal Rule for AI Tasks
Use this instruction at the end of important AI coding prompts:

```text
We are beginner developers.

After completing the task:
1. explain what changed
2. explain why
3. list every file changed
4. give exact test steps
5. mention likely failure cases
6. do not modify unrelated files
7. keep the solution simple enough for us to explain to judges
8. do not claim success unless it can be verified
```
